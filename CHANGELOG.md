# Changelog

## 1.0.3

- **Vomiting:** the stream stays attached to the chin through the whole retching cycle.
- **Loudly Crying / Devastated:** the tear channels follow the eyes and stay attached to the chin, including during head tilts.
- Showcase media re-rendered with the corrected faces.
- Website: link-preview banner for sharing.

## 1.0.2

- README: the two square demos move to the Showcase media section; Mood Wall stays at the top.

## 1.0.1

- README: animated showcase demos (Mood Wall, Big Feelings, Good Company), also shown on the npm page. No icon changes.

## 1.0.0

First public release: 112 animated faces as SVG, still SVG, PNG, GIF and WebP, a GitHub Pages catalogue with copy and download actions, and an npm package.

Refinements before release:

- **Saluting:** a slim, closed-finger hand rests high on the forehead, with a gentle lift and hold.
- **Shushing:** a curved index finger rests at the lips above a rounded, folded hand.
- **Thinking:** the thumb and extended index finger support the jaw from below while the eyes look upward.
- **Celebrating:** a striped party hat, a curled party blower that extends and retracts, and falling confetti.
- Saluting and Celebrating use a small optical offset so their accessories stay inside the 64-unit canvas.
- All faces now loop seamlessly over the 4.8-second cycle.
